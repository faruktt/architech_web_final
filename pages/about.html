About

The escalating complexity of the world and the accelerating speed of change exceed any individual's capacity to comprehend. For architects operating today, the Golden Ratio is no longer the standard - rather, the UN's 17 Sustainable Development Goals are. From a single elegant equation, architects are now held to multidimensional success criteria with almost infinite variables.

Since sustainability is inherently a question of complex systems, circular design, and holistic thinking, no single person holds the solution. As architects and urbanists, we must team with scientists, engineers with biologists, politicians with entrepreneurs, to combine skill sets and perspectives, knowledge and sensibility, to match the complexity of the challenges we face.

BIG has grown organically over the last two decades from a founder, to a family, to a force of 700. Our latest transformation is the BIG LEAP: Bjarke Ingels Group of Landscape, Engineering, Architecture, Planning, and Products. A plethora of in-house perspectives allows us to see what none of us would be able to see on our own. The sum of our individual talents becomes our collective creative genius. A small step for each of us becomes a BIG LEAP for all of us.

Bjarke Ingels
Founder & Creative Director

Landscape

As public space and biodiversity are subtracted in urban spaces, BIG Landscape works with architects and other disciplines to integrate the built and natural worlds, connecting people to people, and people to nature. With every project, we go beyond the brief to give the gift of public space to the communities in which we work, creating equitable and accessible public spaces for all.

+ Public Realm

A new city square Henning Kruses Plads creates a more lively public realm in Denmark's fifth largest city, Esbjerg. The design brings together Esbjerg's old town center and the green city park around the local concert venue Musikhuset.

+ Parks

At the foot of BIG's headquarters in Copenhagen, a former parking area has been transformed into a 1,500 m² public park and promenade, inspired by the sandy beaches and the coastal forests of Denmark.

+ Gardens

Surrounding the UAE Pavilion, meandering paths frame a series of thematic gardens that explore the power of plants as a source of food, health and energy. Throughout the gardens, guests can explore a total of 6,609 plants.

+ Terraces

Located at the intersection of the High Line and the newly developed Hudson Boulevard Park on Manhattan's new western frontier, the terraces at The Spiral visually extend the green space in a spiraling motion towards the sky.

Architecture

We take on the creation of socially, economically and environmentally perfect places as a practical objective. Traditionally, architecture is dominated by two extremes: one that is very wild and expressive, but also expensive and unrealistic, and the other – that is practical and rational, but may also be un-ambitious and uninspiring. We try to occupy the overlap between the two extremes.

+ Culture

The Twist in Kistefos sculpture park is a hybrid spanning several traditional categories: it's a museum, it's a bridge, it's an inhabitable sculpture. As a bridge, it reconfigures the sculpture park, turning the journey through the park into a continuous loop.

+ Education

The density of the urban Arlington neighborhood became the inspiration for the school – BIG fanned the classrooms to maximize daylight and create a courtyard that serves as a social heart for the community.

+ Civic

Public architecture must serve as a catalyst for community identity. Our civic projects balance monumental ambition with intimate human scale, creating spaces that are both iconic and accessible.

+ Residential

From social housing to luxury residences, our residential work explores how people can live better. Projects like 8 House and VIA 57 West demonstrate how density can create community rather than anonymity.

Engineering

BIG Engineering brings technical innovation to the heart of our design process. Our engineers work hand-in-hand with architects from the earliest stages of a project, ensuring that structural, mechanical, and digital systems are not afterthoughts but core elements of the design concept.

+ Structural

Our structural engineering team specializes in complex geometries that push the boundaries of what can be built, from cantilevered volumes to twisted towers and curved roof systems.

+ Sustainability Engineering

Integrating passive solar design, geothermal systems, and renewable energy generation from the earliest design stages ensures our buildings perform as good as they look.

Planning

BIG Planning addresses the large-scale challenges of urbanization, climate change, and social equity through comprehensive master planning and urban design. We believe cities should be designed for people first, and that the built environment can actively contribute to human flourishing and ecological health.

Products

From lamps to sofas, from bicycles to bathroom fittings, BIG Products brings the same design philosophy that drives our architecture to the human scale. Every product emerges from a conceptual investigation into how form, function, and materiality can create something genuinely new.

+ Lighting

Our lighting collaborations with Artemide and Louis Poulsen explore how light shapes space, from the organic form of Gople to the geometric precision of Alphabet of Light.

+ Furniture

Collaborations with HAY, Fritz Hansen, and other leading furniture makers have produced chairs, sofas, and tables that reflect BIG's commitment to the beautiful and the practical.
