People

Founder

Bjarke Ingels

Bjarke Ingels is the founder of BIG. His work is driven by the belief that architecture can be a tool for turning fiction into fact - merging pragmatic solutions with utopian possibilities. Since founding BIG in 2005, Ingels has led projects across architecture, urban design, product design, and research, becoming one of the most recognized architects of his generation. His practice embodies the principle of "hedonistic sustainability" — the idea that a sustainable future must also be an enjoyable one.
