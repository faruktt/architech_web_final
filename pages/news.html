BIG'S TRACEY COFFIN AND PAULINE LAVIE-LUONG REFLECT ON 25 YEARS OF BIG DURING NEW YORK DESIGN WEEK

During this year's DUMBO Design Day – a celebration of the creative Brooklyn neighborhood that over 160 architecture and design firms call home – Two Trees hosted a BIG-focused talk in honor of Phaidon's new monograph, BIG Atlas. The conversation, moderated by Female Design Council Founder Lora Appleton and held on the rooftop of BIG's New York City HQ, brought together BIG's Technical Director Tracey Coffin and Director of Interiors and Associate Pauline Lavie-Luong to reflect on the firm's built work, how Tracey and Pauline lead their respective teams, and their personal journeys at BIG.

'150 BEST HOTELS IN SWITZERLAND' GUIDE NAMES HÔTEL DES HORLOGERS HOTEL OF THE YEAR

EAST SIDE COASTAL RESILIENCY PROJECT WINS AIANY AND ASLANY TRANSPORTATION + INFRASTRUCTURE DESIGN EXCELLENCE AWARD

AIANY and the New York chapter of the American Society of Landscape Architects (ASLA) have awarded the East Side Coastal Resiliency (ESCR) project the Transportation + Infrastructure Design Excellence Award. Designed to protect over 110,000 New Yorkers from future storms while strengthening the neighborhood's connection to the East River, the ribbon of revitalized parkland along Manhattan's east side combines flood protection with public amenities.

HER MAJESTY QUEEN MARGRETHE OF DENMARK PRESENTED WITH BIG'S VISION FOR THE NEW SANKT LUKAS HOSPICE & LUKASHUSET

Her Majesty Queen Margrethe of Denmark visited the Sankt Lukas Foundation to get insight into the Foundation's hospice services and ambitions connected to the new hospice buildings designed by BIG and Creo Arkitekter. The new 8,500 m² palliative care center will house an outreach hospice team, Denmark's first day hospice, and units for children, youth, and adults.

BIG PARTNER AND HEAD OF LANDSCAPE GIULIA FRITTOLI ON THE PRINCIPLES BEHIND GELEPHU MINDFULNESS CITY IN BHUTAN

Bilbao-based newspaper EL CORREO has published a feature on Gelephu Mindfulness City, exploring the project as a new model for future cities. As part of the story, BIG Partner and Head of Landscape Giulia Frittoli discusses her approach to urbanism and the principles behind the project, including the use of local materials and a six-story building height limit designed to keep the city human-scaled and closely connected to its natural surroundings.
